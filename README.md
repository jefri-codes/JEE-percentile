# JEE Percentile Calculator, Rank & College Predictor

A web-based tool that estimates a student's **JEE Main percentile**, predicts an approximate **All India Rank (AIR)**, suggests probable colleges, and maps likely **IIT Madras (IITM) departments** based on the entered JEE Advanced rank.

> **Note:** Predictions are estimates based on previous JoSAA cutoff trends. Actual admission depends on the official JoSAA counselling rounds, category, gender, seat availability, and the candidate's preferences.

## Features

- Calculate approximate **JEE Main percentile** from marks/score input.
- Estimate **JEE Main All India Rank (AIR)** from percentile.
- Predict probable engineering colleges using previous-year cutoff trends.
- Enter a **JEE Advanced AIR** to see which IIT Madras departments may be possible.
- Department-wise prediction for **IIT Madras**.
- Simple, student-friendly interface.
- Useful for early college and branch planning before JoSAA counselling.

## How It Works

1. The student enters their JEE Main marks or score.
2. The application calculates an approximate percentile.
3. Using percentile, it estimates the probable JEE Main AIR.
4. The college predictor compares the estimated rank with previous cutoff data.
5. If the student enters a JEE Advanced AIR, the system shows possible IIT Madras branches based on historical JoSAA closing ranks.

## IIT Madras Department Predictor

IIT Madras admissions are based on the **JEE Advanced AIR**, not the JEE Main percentile. The predictor uses previous JoSAA closing-rank trends to suggest departments that may be within range.

Example reference ranges for the **General / Gender-Neutral** category are shown below. These are only indicative and must not be treated as official cutoffs.

| Approximate JEE Advanced AIR Range | Possible IIT Madras Departments |
|---|---|
| Up to 150 | Computer Science and Engineering |
| 150–250 | Artificial Intelligence and Data Analytics, Electrical Engineering |
| 250–700 | Electrical Engineering |
| 700–1,100 | Computational Engineering and Mechanics, Aerospace Engineering |
| 1,100–1,500 | Mechanical Engineering, Aerospace Engineering |
| 1,500–1,800 | Chemical Engineering, Engineering Physics |
| 1,800–2,500 | Instrumentation and Biomedical Engineering, Biological Engineering |
| 2,500–3,200 | Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering |

For reference, in JoSAA 2026, IIT Madras CSE closed around AIR 151 in the Open/Gender-Neutral category, while Electrical Engineering closed around AIR 681 and Mechanical Engineering around AIR 1,408 in the final round. [1][2]

## Usage

1. Open the application.
2. Enter your JEE Main marks or score.
3. Click **Calculate Percentile**.
4. View your estimated percentile and AIR.
5. Enter your JEE Advanced AIR, if available.
6. View predicted colleges and possible IIT Madras departments.
